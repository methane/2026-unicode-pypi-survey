# Source attribution

Ranking data are from the pinned public hugovk/top-pypi-packages snapshot and its ClickHouse download ranking, identified in the report and data provenance. Package excerpts are from the frozen PyPI source distributions named in each evidence record. Those packages' authors retain their source copyrights and licenses; this analysis does not relicense third-party code.

Exact original sdist URLs, archive SHA-256, member paths and member SHA-256 identify the quoted material. Verified GitHub commit links are supplementary byte/line mappings; missing mappings remain explicit. Short source excerpts are included to substantiate the static classifications. No full third-party source corpus or archives are copied into this publication.

Survey inventory, review, publishing and validation programs are included as reproducible analysis artifacts. Earlier survey publications remain unchanged and are not incorporated as a new source distribution. Publication here means a local report bundle; nothing was uploaded, shared or pushed externally.
